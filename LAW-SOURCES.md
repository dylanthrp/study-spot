Important qualifications: the public-records example refers to local police, whereas federal FOIA covers federal agencies; a state public-records law is the applicable route for local records. The intended multiple-choice label is distinguished from that jurisdictional detail. Judicial witness privilege concerns defamation liability for pertinent testimony, not immunity from perjury prosecution. An accidental injury does not by itself prove all negligence elements. Just compensation is not automatically proportional to acreage or increased by an emotional-attachment multiplier.

## Legal currency

The archived textbook is older. General-rule exercises are framed with appropriate assumptions, and jurisdiction-specific exceptions remain important. This room is an academic study aid, not legal advice or a fully updated legal treatise.

In particular, do not turn old statements about agency deference into current-law answer keys. The Supreme Court overruled Chevron in Loper Bright Enterprises v. Raimondo (2024); under the APA, courts exercise independent judgment on statutory interpretation rather than deferring merely because a statute is ambiguous. This does not eliminate lawful agency discretion or all respect for agency expertise.

Primary update source: https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/23pdf/22-451_7m58.pdf

The PDF also discusses Roe v. Wade as potentially subject to future reversal. That historical discussion must not be represented as current constitutional law. It is not used as a graded current-law rule in this study set.
